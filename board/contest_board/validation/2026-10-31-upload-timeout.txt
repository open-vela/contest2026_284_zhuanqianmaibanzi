Diagnosis reproduction before the fix:
  /tmp/velapoka-upload-diagnose.JomtiK/probe.py
  Real C storage/uploader + C# receiver, 400 ms per 4 KiB BMP read.
  Fast upload and handshake-only controls pass; slow CRC scan fails in 5.72 s.
  Linux error -104, not the NuttX hardware error -107. This reproduces a
  timeout vulnerability, not a proof that all board failures share one cause.

Fix:
  Monotonic 1-second command-phase heartbeats while CRC scanning.
  No heartbeat bytes inserted into raw FILE payloads.
  PC send/receive idle timeout 30 seconds; device I/O poll/reply wait 30 s.
  Device connect wait 3 s; 100 ms cancellation polls; SO_SNDTIMEO 3 s for
  NuttX unbuffered send calls that may wait for ACKs despite O_NONBLOCK.
  Stage retained on error, with filename; device syslog + PC phase errors.
  SD system calls / storage-lock waits are not made cancellable by this fix.
